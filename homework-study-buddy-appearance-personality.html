Rules to Remember
Swipe between slides · hide at any time

Describe appearance 👤

We use be + adjectives to describe people right now or in general:

  • Build: skinny · chubby · overweight · has curves
  • Height: tall · short · medium height
  • Hair: curly hair · straight hair · grey hair
  • Face: beard · moustache

He is tall and has a grey beard.
She has curly hair and beautiful curves.

Describe personality 💬

Personality adjectives are also used with be:

  • outgoing · bubbly · generous · kind → positive
  • rude → negative

My sister is very bubbly and outgoing — she loves parties.

Relationships 🤝

  • get on well (with) — have a good relationship
  • argue — disagree noisily
  • date someone · break up · relationship

👭 My Best Friend

My best friend is called Sara. She is tall and of medium height — okay, okay, just tall! She has long, curly hair and beautiful curves. Her dad is older now, with grey hair and a neat beard.

Personality-wise, Sara is bubbly, outgoing and very generous. She is never rude — she is always kind to everyone. We have a great relationship because we get on well and we almost never argue.

Sara is an influencer, and she dates a photographer called Leo. They are not the kind of couple to break up over small things. In fact, they have been together for three years now. Lucky her!
