🚆 The Overnight Train to Vienna

For years, the idea of travelling from Amsterdam to Vienna entirely by rail had been on the back burner for Diane — always a nice idea, never quite booked. This spring, tempted by the much-heralded new overnight service, she finally went.

She had been hard-pressed to find a ticket in a seated carriage rather than a couchette, but the extra comfort was worth it. As the train left the city, she felt genuinely exhilarated, watching the flat fields whoosh past the window.

Somewhere after Innsbruck, the line dipped through outback towns that most travel guides had completely overlooked — quiet, unhurried places with manicured gardens and church bells instead of traffic.

She found lodging for two nights in a swanky little guesthouse recommended by a fellow passenger, reached by a handy route just a ten-minute walk from the station. It was, she said, the best decision she'd made in years.
